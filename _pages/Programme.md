The workshop will take place in Windhoek from **Monday 26 to Friday 30 October
2026**. The programme below is tentative; final times and rooms will be shared
with participants.

| Date | Theme | Main activities |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Monday 26 October** | Partnership meetings | Introductions and discussions with university, research and agricultural partners |
| **Tuesday 27 October** | Research development | Grant-writing workshop and participant poster session |
| **Wednesday 28 October** | Microbiome research | Research introductions, CABI perspectives, laboratory and field preparation |
| **Thursday 29 October** | Field and data practice | Field visit, data management, data processing and applied research |
| **Friday 30 October** | Analysis and next steps | Microbiome analysis, data visualisation, publishing guidance and closing discussion |

Workshop preparation and software information will be added to the
[Materials page](/resources/) when available.
