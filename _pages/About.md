### Arid-zone Biofertiliser Knowledge Exchange Exercise

**26–30 October 2026**

> Windhoek, Namibia

## Purpose and vision

This five-day workshop will connect microbiome research at Imperial College
London with expertise in arid-zone agriculture in Namibia. The aim is to share
methods, build research capacity and develop new approaches to sustainable crop
production through microbial biofertilisers.

## A collaborative framework

The workshop is a collaboration between Imperial College London, the Namibia
University of Science and Technology (NUST), the University of Namibia (UNAM)
and CABI. It will bring together early-career researchers, principal
investigators and agricultural stakeholders.

## Training and exchange

The programme combines research talks, a grant-writing workshop, poster
discussions, field activity and practical sessions in microbiome data analysis
and visualisation.

## Long-term outcomes

The exchange is intended to support continued collaboration through shared
methods, postgraduate supervision, research visits and future joint funding
applications.

## Supporting projects

The programme builds on [DigiMIC](https://digimic.org/) and
[Microbiome Revolution](https://microbiomerevolution.org/).
