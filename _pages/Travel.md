## Dates and location

The workshop will take place from **26–30 October 2026** in **Windhoek,
Namibia**. The exact venue will be shared with confirmed participants.

## Getting there

International visitors are expected to travel through
[Hosea Kutako International Airport (WDH)](https://www.airports.com.na/airports/3/).
Accommodation and local transport arrangements will be circulated by the
organising team.

## Visas

Entry requirements depend on nationality. International participants should
check the [official Namibian e-services portal](https://eservices.mhaiss.gov.na/).
Invitation letters will be provided where required.

Please wait for confirmation from the organisers before making non-refundable
bookings.
