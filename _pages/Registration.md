  <h3>Preparing your poster</h3>
  <ul class="poster-tips">
    <li>
      Many useful online resources offer advice on creating an effective
      scientific poster. A quick search will provide a wide range of guidance.
    </li>
    <li>
      You have only a few seconds to attract viewers to your work. Clear figures
      paired with concise, easy-to-read text usually have the greatest impact.
    </li>
    <li>
      Avoid long or dense passages of text, including full paragraphs. Make your
      research questions and key findings prominent so readers can identify them
      immediately.
    </li>
    <li>
      Choose font sizes carefully: all text should be legible from a distance of
      at least 1 m.
    </li>
    <li>
      Create a clear visual flow through the poster, normally following the
      natural reading direction from left to right and top to bottom.
    </li>
    <li>
      Include your contact details, including your name and email address.
    </li>
  </ul>

  <p class="poster-guidance__source">
    Guidance adapted from the British Ecological Society poster requirements.
  </p>
