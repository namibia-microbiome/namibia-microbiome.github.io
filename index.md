## Arid-zone biofertiliser knowledge exchange

Taking place in Windhoek from **26–30 October 2026**, this five-day workshop
brings together Imperial College London, the Namibia University of Science and
Technology, the University of Namibia and CABI. It will combine research
exchange, practical training and collaboration in agricultural microbiome
science.

Registration is now open. Visit the [registration page](/registration/) for
key information and a link to the online registration form.
